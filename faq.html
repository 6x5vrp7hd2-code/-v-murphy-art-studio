Frequently asked questions

Are these original paintings?

The Original Collection products are reproductions of paintings made by V. Murphy. Studio Editions are separate, newly developed digital works.

Who fulfils the order?

During launch, enquiries are processed manually. Production supplier, delivery estimate and final total are confirmed before payment.

Can I use a download commercially?

Not under the personal licence. Choose a business/commercial licence and describe the intended use.

Can I request a place that matters to me?

Yes. Studio Editions can be briefed around a location, building, palette, memory or mood, subject to suitable reference material.
