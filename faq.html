Questions

Originals, editions and usage.

A straightforward guide to the collection and the services around it.

What counts as an original V. Murphy work?

Only work actually painted by V. Murphy belongs to the Original Collection.

Does V. Murphy still paint?

No. The archive is finite and will grow online only as the remaining works are catalogued.

Are the catalogue titles original?

Not always. Descriptive working titles are used where the original title is unknown.

What is a Studio Edition?

A contemporary digitally developed composition created by the studio, often from guided customer choices or a reference photograph.

Can a Studio Edition remain private?

Yes, the Private Edition option keeps the final piece out of the standard catalogue, subject to the limitations explained in the legal terms.

Can I print a digital download myself?

Yes, subject to the personal-use licence.

Can I use an image on menus or invitations?

Personal events may be covered by the personal licence; business use requires the appropriate commercial licence.
