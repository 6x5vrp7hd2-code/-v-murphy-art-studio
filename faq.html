About the collection

A straightforward guide to the paintings, catalogue titles, print preparation and purchasing formats.

Are these all original paintings by V. Murphy?

Yes. The works shown are from original paintings by V. Murphy. Reproductions are only released after a suitable digital capture has been prepared.

Are the titles the artist’s original titles?

Not necessarily. Where an original title is unknown, the family uses a simple descriptive catalogue title rather than inventing a story or location.

Are the original paintings for sale?

The present plan is to keep the originals within the family and offer selected works as reproductions. If an original is ever offered, it will be clearly identified as a one-of-a-kind piece.

What formats will be available?

The site is designed around fine art paper prints, selected framed versions, canvas presentations and personal-use digital downloads.

Can I print a digital download myself?

Yes. A personal-use download is intended to allow you to choose your own local printer, paper and frame. The final download listing will state the exact file sizes and permitted uses.

Can I use a download on an invitation or menu?

Personal uses such as invitations, menus, place cards and personal stationery can be included in the download licence. Commercial resale or redistribution would require separate permission.
