Terms, downloads & licensing

Plain-English draft terms for launch. These should be reviewed for your actual business entity, fulfilment arrangements and jurisdiction before taking payments.

1. Copyright and ownership

Purchase of a print, canvas or digital file does not transfer copyright. Copyright and reproduction rights remain with the relevant rights holder. Customers receive only the rights expressly stated with their purchase.

2. Personal digital-download licence

A personal download may be stored by the purchaser and reproduced for private, non-commercial use, including personal wall art, gifts and private-event materials. The file may not be resold, redistributed, uploaded as a stock asset, used in products for sale, used as a business logo/brand asset, or made available to others for reuse.

3. Commercial licences

Commercial use requires a written licence defining the artwork, permitted use, media/product, print run or audience, territory, duration, exclusivity and fee. Any use outside that scope requires a new licence.

4. Studio Editions

Studio Editions are new digitally developed works from V. Murphy Art Studio and are not represented as paintings by V. Murphy. Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, customers receive personal-use rights to the final supplied file, not copyright ownership or unrestricted commercial rights.

5. Customer references and permissions

Customers must have permission to provide photographs, trademarks, addresses, names or other protected material used in a private Studio Edition. The Studio may decline requests that create rights, privacy or suitability concerns.

6. Revisions and approval

The quoted Studio Edition tier includes the stated number of revision rounds. Further changes may be chargeable. Production of physical goods begins only after proof approval.

7. Colour, cropping and production

Screen colour differs from printed colour. Minor cropping may be needed to suit paper, canvas or frame proportions. Final production specifications should be shown at checkout/order confirmation.

8. Cancellations, returns and personalised goods

Insert a policy compliant with the consumer law that applies to your business and customers. Do not publish a blanket “no returns” statement without legal review, particularly for standard physical products.

9. AI / digital production disclosure

Where digital or generative tools contribute to a Studio Edition, the product description should state this clearly. Original Collection reproductions remain separately labelled and should not be described as newly hand-painted works.
