Legal & Usage

Clear boundaries protect the artwork and the customer.

These are practical launch terms for the website and should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before commercial launch.

Launch draft — legal review recommended.

This page is a business working draft, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

1. Copyright in original artwork

Unless expressly transferred in writing, copyright and reproduction rights in V. Murphy artwork remain with the applicable rights holder. Buying a print, frame, canvas or download does not transfer copyright.

2. Personal-use digital downloads

A standard digital download grants a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence for personal use. It may be printed for the purchaser's own home and used in approved personal stationery or events. Files may not be resold, uploaded for others, distributed as source files, or used on commercial merchandise.

3. Commercial licensing

Business use requires a separate written licence stating the permitted artwork, use, media, quantity or print run, territory, duration and any exclusivity. Uses outside that scope require further permission.

4. Studio Editions

Studio Editions are contemporary digital compositions developed by V. Murphy Art Studio. They are not original paintings by V. Murphy. Unless otherwise agreed, the customer receives the finished product or licensed file rather than ownership of the underlying copyright.

5. Private Studio Editions

A Private Edition means the studio will not intentionally add the final composition to its standard catalogue or market that exact final file as a normal edition. Because digitally generated processes can produce visual similarities, absolute worldwide uniqueness cannot be guaranteed unless separately contracted.

6. Customer reference photographs

Customers must have permission to supply and use any photograph submitted for a Studio Edition. The customer must not knowingly submit third-party copyrighted material without authority.

7. Revisions and approval

The number of included revisions is determined by the selected Studio Edition service. Material changes outside the original brief may require an additional quotation.

8. Printing, colour and cropping

Screen colour varies between devices. Physical prints may require small crop adjustments according to the chosen aspect ratio and product format. Customers should review the final proof where one is supplied.

9. Personalisation

Customers are responsible for checking names, dates, wording and spelling before approving personalised products.
