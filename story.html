OUR STORY

Meet V. Murphy

Painting began simply as something she enjoyed.

A personal collection

Over many years, V. Murphy created a personal collection inspired by places, architecture, colour and everyday scenes that caught her eye. The paintings were never intended to become a commercial collection; they were created for the pleasure of painting.

Today, although V. Murphy no longer paints, around 20–30 original works remain. V. Murphy Art Studio was created by her family to preserve that collection, reproduce selected works faithfully, and give them an opportunity to find new homes.

A personal collection, shared for the first time.

The next chapter

The original paintings remain a distinct, finite collection. Alongside them, V. Murphy Studio Editions is a new strand developed by the family: digitally produced pieces, personalised designs and customer-directed artwork inspired by the warmth, places and visual character that sit naturally alongside the collection.

Studio Editions are always identified as new digital works and are never presented as paintings made by V. Murphy. This allows the studio to grow while preserving the authorship and integrity of the originals.
