Behind the paintings

A personal creative life, now gathered into one collection.

Painting was a long-standing hobby for V. Murphy. The work was not originally made for a shop, exhibition programme or commercial catalogue.

Why the studio exists

Preserve first. Sell second.

The purpose of V. Murphy Art Studio is to bring a private body of work together, photograph and catalogue it properly, and make selected images available to people who connect with them.

Where an original title, location or story is unknown, a simple descriptive catalogue title is used. We do not create provenance that cannot be supported.

A finite archive

The original collection will not grow indefinitely.

Because V. Murphy no longer paints, the original collection is naturally limited to the surviving works. New digital compositions may be created by the studio in future, but those belong to the separate Studio Editions collection and are always identified as such.
