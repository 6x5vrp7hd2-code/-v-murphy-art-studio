Behind the paintings

A quiet creative pursuit, built up over years.

Painting was never a commercial venture for V. Murphy. It was a long-standing hobby: time spent observing, experimenting with colour and putting places, buildings and imagined scenes onto paper and canvas.

The collection

Made for the pleasure of making.

Over the years that private pastime became a substantial collection. Some works are gentle architectural studies and street scenes; others are brighter, more playful places or abstract compositions. Together they show a curiosity that was never limited to one subject or style.

V. Murphy Art Studio was created by her family to gather that body of work in one place, preserve the originals and make selected paintings available as carefully produced prints, canvas pieces and digital editions.

An honest catalogue

What we know, and what we do not.

Where the original title, location or story of a painting is unknown, we say so. Descriptive catalogue titles are used to organise the collection rather than to invent a history around the work.

The signed 2013 village scene provides one clear point in the timeline, while the wider archive continues to be photographed and catalogued.
