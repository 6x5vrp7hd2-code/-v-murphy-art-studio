V. Murphy Studio Editions

Create something new, without rewriting the past.

Choose the ingredients of a new composition through a guided process. New pieces are Studio Editions — never presented as paintings by V. Murphy.

Important distinction

Original Collection ≠ Studio Editions

The original V. Murphy collection remains historically fixed. Customer-directed digital compositions belong to a separate studio collection so the provenance of the originals remains clear.

From your photograph

Turn a meaningful place into a studio piece.

A future option can allow customers to submit a photograph of a home, wedding venue, favourite café, family cottage or meaningful street. The studio then interprets the scene using selected style choices.

Any resulting work would be clearly labelled as a contemporary Studio Edition, not an original painting by V. Murphy.

Edition choice

Private or shared?

Studio Edition

You receive the finished piece and the studio may later offer that composition as part of the Studio Editions collection.

Private Edition

The finished composition is not intentionally added to the standard Studio Editions catalogue. Final terms should explain the practical limits of exclusivity for digitally generated imagery.
