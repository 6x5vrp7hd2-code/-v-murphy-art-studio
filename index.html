Behind the paintings

A quiet creative pursuit, built up over years.

Painting was never a commercial venture for V. Murphy. It was a long-standing hobby: time spent observing, experimenting with colour and putting places, buildings and imagined scenes onto paper and canvas.

Over the years that private pastime became a substantial collection. Some works are gentle architectural studies and street scenes; others are brighter, more playful places or abstract compositions. Together they show a curiosity that was never limited to one subject or style.

V. Murphy Art Studio was created by her family to gather that body of work in one place, preserve the originals and make selected paintings available as carefully produced fine art prints.

Where the original title, location or story of a painting is unknown, we say so. Catalogue titles on this site are descriptive names used to organise the collection rather than invented histories.

Questions

About the collection

Are these all original paintings by V. Murphy?

Yes. The collection shown here is drawn from original works by V. Murphy. Prints will only be released after the corresponding original has been photographed and prepared for reproduction.

Are the titles the artist’s original titles?

Not necessarily. Where an original title is unknown, the family uses a simple descriptive catalogue title so that each work can be identified without inventing a story or location.

Are the original paintings for sale?

The present plan is to keep the originals within the family and offer selected works as prints. If that changes, an original will always be clearly identified as a one-of-a-kind work.

Why aren't all the paintings available yet?

The collection is being catalogued gradually. Each work needs a good digital capture, colour correction and a print proof before it is offered for sale.
