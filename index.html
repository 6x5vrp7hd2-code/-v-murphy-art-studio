The original collection

Painted because she enjoyed painting.

V. Murphy painted as a long-standing hobby rather than as a commercial artist. The surviving archive is finite: around 20–30 works created over years of personal practice. The studio exists to preserve, catalogue and share that body of work without inventing history around it.

A clear distinction

The original work stays original.

V. Murphy Original Collection contains only work actually painted by V. Murphy.

V. Murphy Studio Editions contains new digitally developed compositions created by the studio, including customer-directed pieces. They are never presented as paintings by V. Murphy.
